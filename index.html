// HOW THIS ENGINE WORKS

💬 FOR FEEDBACK, QUESTIONS, OR IDEAS, SLACK ERDEM ADIGÜZEL.

1. CADENCE & PIPELINE WORKLOAD

SHOWS HOW MANY ACCOUNTS AND PROSPECTS ARE ACTIVE AT FULL RAMP ONCE YOUR 4-WEEK CADENCE IS RUNNING AT STEADY STATE, AUTOMATICALLY FACTORING IN MID-SEQUENCE DROP-OUTS.

2. DIALS & MULTI-DIALING

CALCULATES YOUR DAILY CALL CAPACITY BASED ON YOUR CALL BLOCKS. PICKUPS TAKE ~10 MINUTES, UNANSWERED CALLS TAKE ~1 MINUTE. BECAUSE YOU HAVE MORE CALL TIME THAN SCHEDULED CONTACTS, MULTI-DIALING (CALLING DESK + MOBILE OR DOUBLE-TAPPING) LETS YOU HIT YOUR DAILY VOLUME COMFORTABLY.

3. ATTAINMENT & PACING

SLIDE WHERE YOU ARE CURRENTLY AT (SQLS BOOKED) AND HOW MANY DAYS ARE LEFT IN THE QUARTER. IT IMMEDIATELY TELLS YOU HOW MANY DIALS AND CALLING MINUTES PER DAY YOU NEED TO HIT BOTH YOUR 100% QUOTA AND 150% STRETCH TARGET.

4. COMMISSION MONETIZATION

BREAKS DOWN YOUR TARGET VARIABLE COMPENSATION SO YOU CAN SEE THE DIRECT GROSS CASH VALUE OF EVERY SINGLE ACTION YOU TAKE—PER RAW DIAL, PER LIVE CONVERSATION, PER EMAIL, AND PER LINKEDIN TOUCH.
